# Access and Password Policy

- Owner: Information Security
- Applies to: all employees and contractors
- Version: 2.3
- Binding: yes

## ACC-01 – Accounts

Every person gets a personal account. Sharing accounts or passwords is not
allowed, also not within a team. Technical accounts for services are
requested via the IT service desk and have an owner team.

## ACC-02 – Passwords

- Minimum length: **14 characters**. There are no other complexity rules.
- Use the company password manager to generate and store passwords.
- Passwords are not changed periodically, only when there is a suspicion
  that they are known to someone else.
- Never reuse a company password for private services.

## ACC-03 – Multi-factor authentication

MFA is mandatory for all company accounts. Use the authenticator app or a
hardware key; SMS is not allowed as a second factor.

## ACC-04 – Least privilege

Access rights are granted based on the role and only as long as they are
needed. Production access for developers is granted temporarily
(**maximum 8 hours**) via the access portal and must be justified with a
ticket.

## ACC-05 – Access reviews

Team leads review the access rights of their team members **every 6
months**. Rights that are no longer needed are revoked.

## ACC-06 – Leaving the company

All accounts are deactivated on the last working day at 18:00. Laptops,
hardware keys and access cards are returned to the IT service desk.

## ACC-07 – Screen lock

Lock your screen whenever you leave your device (`Win + L` / `Ctrl + Cmd + Q`).
Devices lock automatically after 5 minutes.
