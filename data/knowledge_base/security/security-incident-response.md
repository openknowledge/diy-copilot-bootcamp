# Security Incident Response

- Owner: Information Security
- Applies to: all employees
- Version: 1.8

A security incident is any event that may compromise the confidentiality,
integrity or availability of our data or systems. When in doubt, report it –
false alarms are not a problem.

## IR-01 – How to report

Report security incidents immediately in `#security` in Slack or by calling
the security hotline **+49 30 1234 5678** (24/7). Do not try to investigate
or fix the incident yourself before reporting it.

## IR-02 – Lost or stolen devices

If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen:

1. Call the security hotline immediately – the device will be locked and
   wiped remotely.
2. Report the theft to the police and send the police report to the IT
   service desk.
3. Change the password of your company account from another device.

## IR-03 – Phishing

Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails. Use the
"Report phishing" button in the mail client. If you already entered your
password on a phishing site, change it immediately and inform `#security`.

## IR-04 – Malware

If you suspect malware on your device, disconnect it from the network
(Wi-Fi off, cable out) and call the security hotline. Do not switch the
device off.

## IR-05 – Severity levels

| Level | Example | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Customer data leaked, ransomware | 15 minutes |
| High | Compromised employee account | 1 hour |
| Medium | Lost device (encrypted) | 4 hours |
| Low | Phishing email reported, not clicked | 1 working day |

## IR-06 – After an incident

Every incident with severity high or critical gets a blameless post-incident
review within 5 working days.
