# Event Spaces Policy

- Owner: Facility Management / Internal Communications
- Applies to: events in the Berlin office
- Version: 1.1

Besides the meeting rooms, the Berlin office has two event spaces: the
**rooftop terrace** (up to 80 people, April to October) and the **canteen**
(up to 150 people, after 15:00). Event spaces are not booked with Roomie but
via the event request form in the intranet.

## 1. What counts as an event

An event is a gathering with more than 16 people, external guests or
catering beyond coffee and water – e.g. all-hands meetings, team parties,
meetups or customer events. Smaller meetings use the meeting rooms (see the
[booking policy](room-booking-policy.md)).

## 2. Requesting an event space

- Requests must be submitted at least **4 weeks in advance**.
- Events can last up to **8 hours**, including set-up and clean-up.
- Events on weekends are possible for the rooftop terrace and need the
  approval of the facility management.
- Internal communications confirms the request within 3 working days.

## 3. Catering and cleaning

Catering is **mandatory** for events in the canteen and is ordered by
facility management based on the request. After every event, the cleaning
team needs **2 hours**; no other event can take place during this time.

## 4. Cancellation

Event requests can be cancelled free of charge until **5 working days**
before the event. Later cancellations are charged to the cost centre of the
organiser (catering costs).

## 5. Noise and safety

Music must end at 22:00. The organiser names one person responsible for
safety, who stays until all guests have left.
