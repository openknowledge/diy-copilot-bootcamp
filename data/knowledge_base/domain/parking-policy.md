# Parking Policy Berlin

- Owner: Facility Management
- Applies to: all employees of the Berlin office
- Version: 2.0

The underground car park of the Berlin office has 40 parking spaces, 6 of
them with a charging station for electric cars. Parking spaces are booked
with the app "ParkPal".

## 1. Booking a parking space

- Parking spaces can be booked at most **7 days in advance**.
- A parking space is booked for a whole day (06:00–22:00).
- Every employee can hold at most **2 future bookings** at the same time.
- Spaces with a charging station can only be booked by drivers of electric
  or plug-in hybrid cars.

## 2. Check-in

The licence plate is recognised at the barrier. Your booking counts as
checked in when you enter the car park. If you have not entered the car park
by **10:00**, the booking is released and the space can be booked by others
for the rest of the day.

## 3. Cancellation

Bookings can be cancelled in ParkPal until **08:00 on the same day**. Later
cancellations and bookings that were released because nobody arrived count
as a **no-show**. Employees with more than 3 no-shows in a month cannot book
parking spaces for the following month.

## 4. Charging

Charging is free of charge. Please move your car after charging so that
colleagues can charge too; the maximum charging time is **4 hours**. Cars
blocking a charging station longer than that may be moved to a normal space
by the facility team.

## 5. Bicycles

Bicycle parking in the courtyard does not need to be booked. E-bike
batteries may be charged at the lockers on floor 0.

## 6. Guests

Parking for guests is booked by the host via the facility team at least one
working day in advance.
