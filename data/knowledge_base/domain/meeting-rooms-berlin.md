# Meeting Rooms in Berlin

- Owner: Facility Management
- Last updated: February 2026

## Room names and ids

Every room has a name (Berlin lakes, rivers and districts) and an id. The id
follows the pattern `<building><floor>-<number>`: `B2-14` is room number 14 on
floor 2 of building B. Always use the id when you refer to a room in tickets
or in code, names can change.

## Video equipment

Rooms with video have a ceiling microphone, a 75" screen and a conference
camera. Start a call by connecting the USB-C cable to your laptop. The system
supports all common video tools.

## Catering

Catering is available in rooms marked with "Catering = yes" and must be
ordered at least **2 working days in advance** via the catering form in the
intranet. The booking system does not order catering. Please remember the
cleaning time of 15 minutes after meetings with catering (see the
[booking policy](room-booking-policy.md)).

Coffee and water are available in every kitchen and don't need to be ordered.

## Accessibility

All rooms in building A are accessible by elevator. Room A2-10 has an
induction loop for hearing aids.

## Problems in a room

Report broken equipment, missing chairs or a broken door display to the
facility team via `#facility-roomie`. If the door display of your room is
broken, check in via Slack with `/room checkin <booking id>`.
