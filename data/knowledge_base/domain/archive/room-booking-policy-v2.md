- Owner: Facility Management
- Applies to: all employees of the Berlin office
- Version: 2.4, valid from 1 April 2023 until 31 December 2024

## 1. Booking times

Bookings start and end on a full or half hour. A booking lasts at least
30 minutes and at most **3 hours**. Rooms can be booked from Monday to
Friday between 08:00 and 19:00, at most **90 days in advance**.

## 2. Choosing the right room

The number of attendees must not exceed the number of seats. Rooms with
10 or more seats should preferably be used for larger groups, but there is
no minimum number of attendees.

## 3. Check-in

Bookings must be confirmed at the door display within **30 minutes** after
the start. Unconfirmed bookings are released.

## 4. Cancellation

Bookings can be cancelled until **1 hour before the start**. Cancelled
bookings are deleted from the system after 4 weeks.

## 5. Catering

Catering must be ordered 1 working day in advance. Rooms with catering are
cleaned during the next booking slot if necessary.
