# Meeting Rooms in Munich – Booking Rules

- Owner: Facility Management Munich
- Applies to: all employees and guests of the Munich office
- Version: 1.3, valid from 1 March 2026

The Munich office is smaller than the Berlin office and shares its building
with other companies. Therefore some booking rules differ from the
[Berlin booking policy](room-booking-policy.md). Meeting rooms in Munich are
booked with the building's booking system "SpaceHub", not with Roomie.

## 1. Booking times

- Rooms can be booked from **Monday to Friday between 08:00 and 18:00**.
  The building is closed outside these hours.
- A booking lasts at least 30 minutes and at most **2 hours**. Longer
  meetings need the approval of the office manager.
- Bookings always start on a full or half hour (e.g. 09:00 or 09:30).
- Rooms can be booked at most **30 days in advance**.

There is no big room in Munich. For all-hands meetings, the conference
centre on the ground floor of the building can be rented via the office
manager.

## 3. Check-in and no-shows

Bookings must be checked in on the tablet next to the door within
**15 minutes** after the start. If nobody checks in, the booking is cancelled
automatically and the room is available for all companies in the building.

## 4. Cancellation

Bookings can be cancelled in SpaceHub until **30 minutes before the start**.
Later cancellations are not possible; please release the room on the tablet
at the door instead.

## 5. Catering

Catering is provided by the building's canteen and must be ordered **one
working day in advance** until 12:00. There is no cleaning buffer between
bookings; please leave the room clean.
