# Meeting Room Booking Policy

- Owner: Facility Management
- Applies to: all employees and guests of the Berlin office
- Version: 3.2, valid from 1 January 2026

Meeting rooms are a shared and limited resource. This policy defines how rooms
are booked, used and released so that everyone finds a room when they need
one. The rules are enforced by the booking system "Roomie".

## 1. Who can book

All employees can book meeting rooms with their personal account. Guests
cannot book rooms themselves; their host books the room for them.

Members of the facility team have the role **facility admin**. Facility admins
can create and change rooms, cancel any booking at any time and book rooms on
behalf of others, e.g. for company events.

## 2. Booking times

### 2.1 Time grid

Bookings always start and end on a full quarter hour (e.g. 09:00, 09:15,
09:30, 09:45). A meeting from 10:10 to 10:50 is not possible; book 10:00 to
11:00 instead. The system does not round times automatically.

### 2.2 Duration

A booking lasts at least 15 minutes and at most **4 hours**. Rooms cannot be
blocked for a whole day by a single booking. Workshops that need a room for a
full day are booked by the facility team as several consecutive bookings –
please send a request to the facility team at least one week in advance.

### 2.3 Business hours

Rooms can be booked from **Monday to Friday between 07:00 and 20:00** (Berlin
time). A booking must start and end on the same day. There are no bookings on
weekends; for weekend events contact the facility team.

### 2.4 Booking in advance

Rooms can be booked at most **60 days in advance**. Bookings further in the
future are not possible, because the room plan may change (e.g. renovations).

## 3. Choosing the right room

### 3.1 Capacity

The number of attendees must not exceed the number of seats of the room.

### 3.2 Big room policy

Rooms with **12 or more seats** may only be booked for meetings with **at
least 4 attendees**. Our big rooms are frequently requested for all-hands,
trainings and customer workshops – small meetings block them unnecessarily.
For 1:1 meetings and small groups please use the small rooms on floor 1 or the
focus boxes. Exceptions are not granted, not even for customer calls; the
small rooms A1-01 and A1-02 are equipped with the same video system.

### 3.3 Rooms with catering

Some rooms can be booked with catering. After every meeting in such a room the
cleaning team needs **15 minutes** to clean up. During this time the room is
blocked, so the next booking can start 15 minutes after the previous one ends
at the earliest. Catering itself is ordered separately (see
[Meeting rooms in Berlin](meeting-rooms-berlin.md)).

## 4. Check-in and no-shows

### 4.1 Check-in

Every booking must be checked in by the person who booked it. Check-in is
possible from **5 minutes before** until **10 minutes after** the start of
the meeting, on the display next to the door or with the Slack command
`/room checkin`.

### 4.2 Automatic release

If nobody checks in within 10 minutes after the start, the booking is
**released automatically** and the room is free for others. The person who
booked it receives an email, and the facility team is informed. A released
booking cannot be checked in anymore – book the room again if it is still
free.

This rule also applies to bookings of facility admins.

> Background: before the introduction of the check-in, about 30% of all
> booked meetings never took place while other colleagues searched for a room.

## 5. Cancellation

Please cancel bookings you don't need as early as possible.

- You can cancel your own bookings yourself until **2 hours before the
  start**.
- Later cancellations are only possible by the facility team. Contact them
  via `#facility-roomie` in Slack.
- Cancelled bookings are not deleted. They are kept for statistics about room
  usage (see [Data protection requirements](../security/data-protection.md)).

## 6. Misuse

Repeated no-shows or bookings "just in case" are evaluated monthly. Teams with
a no-show rate above 25% are contacted by facility management.
