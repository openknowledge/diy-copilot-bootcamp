# Business Travel and Expenses

- Owner: Finance
- Applies to: all employees
- Version: 4.1

## 1. Approval

Business trips must be approved by the team lead before booking. Trips
abroad additionally need the approval of the department head.

## 2. Booking

- Trains are booked in 2nd class; for trips longer than 4 hours 1st class is
  allowed.
- Flights within Germany are only allowed if the train ride takes longer
  than 6 hours.
- Hotels up to **€150 per night** (Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt: €180) are
  booked via the travel portal.

## 3. Daily allowance

For trips within Germany, the daily allowance is **€14** for absences of more
than 8 hours and **€28** for a full day. Meals paid by the company (e.g. at a
customer workshop) reduce the allowance.

## 4. Expenses

Receipts are uploaded to the expense app within **30 days** after the trip.
Expenses without receipts are not reimbursed. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## 5. Conferences

Conference tickets up to €1,000 are approved by the team lead; more
expensive tickets need the approval of the department head. Every employee
has an annual training budget of **€2,000**.

## 6. Car

Private cars are only used if public transport is not reasonable. The
mileage allowance is €0.30 per kilometre.
