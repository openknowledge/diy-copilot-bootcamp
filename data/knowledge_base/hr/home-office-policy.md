# Home Office Policy

- Owner: People & Culture
- Applies to: all employees in Germany
- Version: 3.0

## 1. Mobile work

Employees can work from home or another place in Germany up to **3 days per
week**, in agreement with their team. Teams agree on at least one common
office day per week.

## 2. Working from abroad

Working from another EU country is possible for up to **20 working days per
year** and must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR portal.
Working from outside the EU is not possible for tax and data protection
reasons.

## 3. Equipment

Employees receive a one-time allowance of **€500** for home office equipment
(e.g. chair, monitor) and can borrow a monitor and a docking station from the
IT service desk. Company laptops must not be used by family members.

## 4. Working hours and availability

The core working hours are 10:00–15:00. Outside core hours, employees are
not expected to answer messages. Overtime is recorded in the HR portal.

## 5. Data protection at home

Confidential documents must not be printed at home. Make sure nobody can see
your screen during calls with customer data. Use the company VPN for all
work.

## 6. Accidents

Accidents while working from home are covered by the statutory accident
insurance if they are directly related to work. Report them to HR within
3 days.
